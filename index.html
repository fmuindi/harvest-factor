From the Editor’s Desk

The Fields Are White Already to Harvest

There is a field, and it is ready. Not tomorrow — now. Every evangelist who has ever knelt at an altar and asked God for the nations knows the weight of this hour: the harvest does not wait for the comfortable, it waits for the sent. Harvest Factor exists for one reason — to gather the soul-winners of this generation into one room, one mandate, one fire.

This is not a conference to attend. It is a mobilization to join. For ten days, evangelists, street preachers, missionaries, and everyday believers with a hunger for souls will assemble — not to be entertained, but to be equipped, armed, and sent back to the harvest field with greater fire than they arrived with.

We will pray like intercessors. We will study like theologians. We will preach like it is the last altar call this city will ever get. Because for someone, it will be.

We are not gathering an audience. We are mobilizing an army of soul-winners for the last great harvest. — The Call of Harvest Factor
